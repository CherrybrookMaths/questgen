        Original data set (6 scores): the median is the mean of the 3rd and 4th scores.
        $$\begin{aligned} \text{median} &= \dfrac{7+9}{2} = 8 \\ \text{mean} &= \dfrac{4+7+7+9+12+15}{6} = \dfrac{54}{6} = 9 \end{aligned}$$
