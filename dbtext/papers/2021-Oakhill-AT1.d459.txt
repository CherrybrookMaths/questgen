        Intersections occur where the two $y$ values are equal:
        $$\begin{aligned} x^2-3x+2 &= 2x+b \\ x^2-5x+(2-b) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$

        $$\begin{aligned} \Delta &= (-5)^2-4(1)(2-b) \\ &= 17+4b \end{aligned}$$

        A quadratic equation cannot have three solutions.
