        $$\begin{aligned} y&=ax^2+bx+c\\ y'&=2ax+b\end{aligned}$$
        The gradient of the tangent and the curve is the same at $(-1,0)$:
        $$\begin{aligned} 2a(-1)+b&=5\\ -2a+b&=5 &&(1)\end{aligned}$$
        The maximum turning point is at $x=\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $y'\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=0$:
        $$\begin{aligned} 2a\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+b&=0\\ \dfrac{a}{2}+b&=0 &&(2)\end{aligned}$$
        Subtracting (1) from (2):
        $$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{a}{2}+2a&=-5\\ \dfrac{5a}{2}&=-5\\ a&=-\dfrac{5\times2}{5}=-2\end{aligned}$$
        From (2):
        $$b=-\dfrac{a}{2}=1$$
        For $c$, substitute the point $(-1,0)$, $a$ and $b$ into $y=ax^2+bx+c$:
        $$\begin{aligned} 0&=-2(-1)^2+1(-1)+c\\ c-1-2&=0\\ c&=3\end{aligned}$$
        $$\therefore\ a=-2,\ b=1,\ c=3$$
