        The water is safe when $h<4$. Find when $h=4$.
        $$\begin{aligned}5+2\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi t}{4}\right)&=4\\\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi t}{4}\right)&=-\dfrac{1}{2}\end{aligned}$$
        Sine is negative in the third and fourth quadrants, so
        $$\dfrac{\pi t}{4}=\dfrac{7\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{19\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{23\pi}{6},\ \ldots$$
        Multiplying by $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$:
        $$t=\dfrac{14}{3},\ \dfrac{22}{3},\ \dfrac{38}{3},\ \dfrac{46}{3},\ \ldots$$
        At 12 pm ($t=12$), $h=5>4$, and the first solution with $t\ge 12$ is $t=\dfrac{38}{3}=12\dfrac{2}{3}$ hours. After this the height is below $4$ m.
        $$\therefore\ \text{the earliest time is } 12{:}40\text{ pm}$$
