      latex: |
        $$\begin{aligned} y&=x^3-3x^2-9x+4 \\ y'&=3x^2-6x-9 \\ &=3(x^2-2x-3) \\ &=3(x-3)(x+1) \end{aligned}$$
        $$\begin{aligned} y''&=6x-6 \\ &=6(x-1) \end{aligned}$$
        For stationary points, $y'=0$:
