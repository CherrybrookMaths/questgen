      Hence the correct option is D.

        The loan is repaid when $A_n=0$, with $P=320$.
        $$\begin{aligned}20000\times1.004^n-\dfrac{320(1.004^n-1)}{0.004}&=0\\20000(1.004)^n&=80000(1.004^n-1)\\60000(1.004)^n&=80000\\(1.004)^n&=\dfrac{4}{3}\end{aligned}$$
        Take logarithms.
        $$\begin{aligned}n&=\dfrac{\ln\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)}{\ln(1.004)}\\&\approx72.06\end{aligned}$$
        Since a whole number of instalments is needed, the loan is paid completely after 73 months.
