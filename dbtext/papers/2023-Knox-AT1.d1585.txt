      The probabilities sum to 1:
      $$r+r^2+r^3+\dots+r^{n-1}+r^n=1$$
      This is a geometric series with $a=r$, ratio $r$ and $n$ terms:
      $$S_n=\dfrac{r(r^n-1)}{r-1}=1$$
      $$\begin{aligned} r^{n+1}-r &= r-1 \\ r^{n+1} &= 2r-1 \end{aligned} \qquad (1)$$
      The expected value pairs each value with its probability:
      $$\begin{aligned} E(X) &= \sum xP(X=x) \\ &= r\cdot r^n+r^2\cdot r^{n-1}+r^3\cdot r^{n-2}+\dots+r^n\cdot r \\ &= r^{n+1}+r^{n+1}+\dots+r^{n+1} \\ &= nr^{n+1} \end{aligned}$$
      Using (1):
      $$E(X)=n(2r-1)$$
