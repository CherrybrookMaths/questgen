        The depth is at most $11$ m when $h(t)\le 11$. First solve $h(t)=11$.
        $$\begin{aligned}3\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{6}+13&=11 \\ \cos\dfrac{\pi t}{6}&=-\dfrac{2}{3}\end{aligned}$$
        The reference angle is $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{2}{3}\approx 0.8411$, and cosine is negative in the second and third quadrants. For the first cycle, $0\le t\le 12$:
        $$\begin{aligned}\dfrac{\pi t}{6}&\approx \pi-0.8411,\ \pi+0.8411 \\ &\approx 2.3005,\ 3.9827 \\ t&\approx 4.394,\ 7.606 \\ &\approx 4\text{ h }24\text{ min},\ 7\text{ h }36\text{ min}\end{aligned}$$
        Since $t$ is the number of hours after 4 am, add 4 hours:
        $$\begin{aligned}4{:}24+4\text{ h}&=8{:}24\text{ am} \\ 7{:}36+4\text{ h}&=11{:}36\text{ am}\end{aligned}$$
        Between these times $\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{6}\le-\dfrac{2}{3}$, so $h\le 11$.
        $$\therefore\ \text{the family can be at the beach from } 8{:}24\text{ am until } 11{:}36\text{ am}$$
