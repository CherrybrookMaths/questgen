      The tree starts at $1.2$ m. Each year's growth is $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the previous year's, so the yearly growths form a geometric series with $r = \dfrac{3}{8}$.

      The first year's growth is
      $$ar = 1.2 \times \dfrac{3}{8} = 0.45$$
      and the second year's growth is
      $$ar^2 = 0.16875$$
      and so on.

      Since $|r| < 1$, the sum to infinity exists. The total future growth is
      $$\begin{aligned} S_\infty &= \dfrac{0.45}{1 - \frac{3}{8}} \\ &= 0.72 \end{aligned}$$
      So the eventual height is
      $$1.2 + 0.72 = 1.92 \text{ m}$$
      Equivalently, with $a = 1.2$ and $r = \dfrac{3}{8}$:
      $$\begin{aligned} S_\infty &= \dfrac{a}{1 - r} \\ &= \dfrac{1.2}{1 - \frac{3}{8}} \\ &= 1.92 \end{aligned}$$
      So the correct option is C.
