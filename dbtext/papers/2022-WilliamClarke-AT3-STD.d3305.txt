        Compound interest formula: $A = P\left(1+r\right)^n$, where $r$ is the rate per compounding period and $n$ is the number of periods.

        Options A and D use the annual rate $5.2\%$ per quarter, and option B uses only $4$ periods, so they are incorrect.
