    latex: |
      Solve $x+y=10$ and $2x-3y=10$ simultaneously. Substituting $y=10-x$:

      $$\begin{aligned} 2x-3(10-x) &= 10 \\ 5x-30 &= 10 \\ x &= 8,\ y=2 \end{aligned}$$

      Line $x+y=10$ has intercepts $(10,0)$ and $(0,10)$, so it is the line with negative gradient, $g$. Line $2x-3y=10$ has gradient $\dfrac{2}{3}$, so it is $f$.

      Hence $f$: $2x-3y=10$, $g$: $x+y=10$, with solution $x=8$, $y=2$, which is option D.
