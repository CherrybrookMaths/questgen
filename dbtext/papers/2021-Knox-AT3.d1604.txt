      latex: |
        From the graph, the curves meet at $x=1$ and $x=2$. Check: $\dfrac{2}{x} = -x+3$ gives $x^2-3x+2=0$, so $x=1$ or $x=2$.

        For $1\le x\le 2$ the line is above the curve. For $2\le x\le 4$ the curve is above the line. So the area is
        $$A=\int_1^2\left(-x+3-\dfrac{2}{x}\right)dx+\int_2^4\left(\dfrac{2}{x}+x-3\right)dx$$
        First integral:
        $$\begin{aligned} \left[-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x-2\ln x\right]_1^2 &= (-2+6-2\ln 2)-\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}+3\right) \\ &= \dfrac{3}{2}-2\ln 2 \end{aligned}$$
        Second integral:
        $$\begin{aligned} \left[2\ln x+\dfrac{x^2}{2}-3x\right]_2^4 &= (2\ln 4+8-12)-(2\ln 2+2-6) \\ &= 4\ln 2-4-2\ln 2+4 \\ &= 2\ln 2 \end{aligned}$$
        Adding,
        $$A=\dfrac{3}{2}-2\ln 2+2\ln 2$$
        $$\therefore\ A=\dfrac{3}{2}\text{ units}^2$$
