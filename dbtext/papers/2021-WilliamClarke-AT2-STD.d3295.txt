        Let $h_1$ be the height of the first section and $x$ the total height. Use the tangent ratio in each right-angled triangle.
        $$\tan 25^\circ = \dfrac{h_1}{87}$$
        $$\begin{aligned} h_1 &= 87\tan 25^\circ \\ &\approx 40.5687 \end{aligned}$$
        $$\tan 40^\circ = \dfrac{x}{87}$$
        $$\begin{aligned} x &= 87\tan 40^\circ \\ &\approx 73.0016 \end{aligned}$$
        The second section is the difference.
        $$\begin{aligned} h_2 &= 73.0016 - 40.5687 \\ &\approx 32.43 \\ &\approx 32 \text{ m} \end{aligned}$$
