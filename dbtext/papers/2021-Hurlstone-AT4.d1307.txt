    latex: |
      For a point of inflection, $f''(x)=0$ and the concavity changes.

      Differentiating:
      $$\begin{aligned} f'(x)&=6x^2+24x+6 \\ f''(x)&=12x+24 \end{aligned}$$
      Setting $f''(x)=0$:
      $$\begin{aligned} 12x+24&=0 \\ x&=-2 \end{aligned}$$
      Since $f''(x)$ is linear, it changes sign at $x=-2$.

      Hence the answer is option B, $x=-2$.
