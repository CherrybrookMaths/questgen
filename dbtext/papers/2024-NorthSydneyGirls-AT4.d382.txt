        The curve passes through the origin, so the region runs from $x=0$ to $x=1$. Using part (b), with area $\dfrac{1}{m}$:
        $$\begin{aligned} A &= \int_0^1 xe^{mx}\,dx = \dfrac{1}{m} \\ \dfrac{1}{m}\left[e^{mx}\left(x-\dfrac{1}{m}\right)\right]_0^1 &= \dfrac{1}{m} \\ e^m\left(1-\dfrac{1}{m}\right)-e^0\left(0-\dfrac{1}{m}\right) &= 1 &&(\text{multiply by }m) \\ e^m\left(1-\dfrac{1}{m}\right)+\dfrac{1}{m}-1 &= 0 \\ e^m\left(1-\dfrac{1}{m}\right)-\left(1-\dfrac{1}{m}\right) &= 0 \\ \left(1-\dfrac{1}{m}\right)\left(e^m-1\right) &= 0 \end{aligned}$$
        So $1-\dfrac{1}{m}=0$ or $e^m=1$, giving $m=1$ or $m=0$. But $m\ne 0$, since the area is $\dfrac{1}{m}$.
        $$\therefore\ m=1$$
