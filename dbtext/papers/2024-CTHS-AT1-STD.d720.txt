        Drawing with replacement gives $6\times 6 = 36$ equally likely outcomes. Count the outcomes for each option.

        Option A: sum of 5.
        $$(1,4),\ (2,3),\ (3,2),\ (4,1)$$

        Option B: one odd and one even. There are 3 odd and 3 even numbers, and either order is allowed.

        $$(1,1),\ (2,2),\ \dots,\ (6,6)$$

        Each can be reversed, giving 8 outcomes, so $P = \dfrac{8}{36}$.

        The smallest probability is $\dfrac{4}{36}$, so the least likely outcome is the sum of 5.
