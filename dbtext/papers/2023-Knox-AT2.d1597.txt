      Translating $b$ units left gives $8(x+b)^2$.

      Dilating horizontally by factor $a$ replaces $x$ with $\dfrac{x}{a}$, giving $8\left(\dfrac{x}{a}+b\right)^2$.

      Translating $c$ units up gives $8\left(\dfrac{x}{a}+b\right)^2+c$.

      Equate to $g(x)$ and expand:

      $$8\left(\dfrac{x}{a}+b\right)^2+c\equiv 2x^2+4x+21$$

      $$\dfrac{8}{a^2}x^2+\dfrac{16b}{a}x+(8b^2+c)\equiv 2x^2+4x+21$$

      Equating coefficients of $x^2$:

      $$\dfrac{8}{a^2}=2$$

      so $a=2$, since $a>0$.

      Equating coefficients of $x$:

      $$\dfrac{16b}{a}=4$$

      so $b=\dfrac{1}{2}$.

      Equating constants: $8b^2+c=21$, so $c=19$.

      $$\therefore\ a=2,\ b=\dfrac{1}{2},\ c=19$$

      The alternative working with a translation $b$ to the right gives $a=2$, $b=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, $c=19$.
