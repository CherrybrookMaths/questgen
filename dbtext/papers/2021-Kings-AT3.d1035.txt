        Rewrite the equation as

        $$y=\dfrac{4}{x-1}-2$$

        This is $y=\dfrac{4}{x}$ shifted 1 unit right and 2 units down, so the asymptotes are $x=1$ and $y=-2$.

        Find the intercepts.

        When $x=0$:

        $$y=\dfrac{4}{-1}-2=-6$$

        When $y=0$:

        $$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{4}{x-1}&=2 \\ x-1&=2 \\ x&=3 \end{aligned}$$

        Only graph B has these intercepts, with the horizontal asymptote $y=-2$ (the other graphs have asymptotes at $y=2$ or $y=-2$ but pass through $(0,0)$ or $(0,4)$).

        The answer is B.
