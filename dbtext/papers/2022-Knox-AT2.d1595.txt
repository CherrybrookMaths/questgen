      latex: |
        Differentiate, then stationary points occur when $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=0$.
        $$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 12x - 3x^2$$
        $$\begin{aligned} 0 &= 12x - 3x^2 \\ &= 3x(4-x) \end{aligned}$$
        So $x = 0$ or $x = 4$.

        When $x=0$, $y=0$.
        $$\begin{aligned} \text{When } x=4,\ y &= 6\times 4^2 - 4^3 \\ &= 32 \end{aligned}$$
        \extable{Ts4660_1}

        The slope is decreasing, flat, increasing, flat, decreasing, so there is a maximum turning point at $(4,32)$ and a minimum turning point at $(0,0)$.

        Alternatively, use the second derivative.
        $$\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 12 - 6x$$
        At $x=0$, $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 12>0$ (concave up), so a minimum.

        At $x=4$, $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -12<0$ (concave down), so a maximum.
        $$\therefore\ \text{maximum at } (4,32),\ \text{minimum at } (0,0)$$
