        Expanding the area from part (i):
        $$A=4\sin\theta+4\sin\theta\cos\theta$$
        Using the product rule, $\dfrac{d}{d\theta}(uv)=u'v+uv'$, on $4\sin\theta\cos\theta$, with
        $$\begin{aligned}u&=4\sin\theta & v&=\cos\theta\\u'&=4\cos\theta & v'&=-\sin\theta\end{aligned}$$
        $$\begin{aligned}\dfrac{dA}{d\theta}&=4\cos\theta+4\sin\theta(-\sin\theta)+4\cos\theta\cos\theta\\&=4\cos\theta-4(1-\cos^2\theta)+4\cos^2\theta\\&=8\cos^2\theta+4\cos\theta-4\end{aligned}$$
        For a maximum or minimum, $\dfrac{dA}{d\theta}=0$:
        $$\begin{aligned}4(2\cos^2\theta+\cos\theta-1)&=0\\(2\cos\theta-1)(\cos\theta+1)&=0\end{aligned}$$
