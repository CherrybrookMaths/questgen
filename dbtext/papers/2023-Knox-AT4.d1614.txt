      The curve $y=\dfrac{1}{2x}$ is decreasing, so the lower rectangles have area less than the integral and the upper rectangles have area greater than it.

      The heights are $y=\dfrac{1}{2}$ at $x=1$, $y=\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $x=1.5$ and $y=\dfrac{1}{4}$ at $x=2$.

      Lower rectangles:
      $$\dfrac{1}{3}\times\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}\times\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{7}{24}$$
      Upper rectangles:
      $$\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}\times\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{5}{12}$$
      The exact area is
      $$\begin{aligned} \int_1^2\dfrac{dx}{2x} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left[\ln x\right]_1^2 \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\ln2-\ln1) \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2}\ln2 \\ &= \ln\sqrt2 \end{aligned}$$
      Hence
      $$\dfrac{7}{24}<\ln\sqrt2<\dfrac{5}{12}$$
