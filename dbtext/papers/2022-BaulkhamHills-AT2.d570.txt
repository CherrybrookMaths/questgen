        Points of inflexion occur where $y''=0$:
        $$e^{\sin x}\left(\cos^2 x - \sin x\right) = 0$$
        Since $e^{\sin x} > 0$, $\cos^2 x - \sin x = 0$. Using $\cos^2 x = 1-\sin^2 x$:
        $$\begin{aligned} 1 - \sin^2 x - \sin x &= 0 \\ \sin^2 x + \sin x - 1 &= 0 \\ \sin x &= \dfrac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2} \end{aligned}$$
        $\dfrac{-1-\sqrt{5}}{2}<-1$ has no solution, so $\sin x=\dfrac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$.
