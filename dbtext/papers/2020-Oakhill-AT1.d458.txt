    latex: |
      The function is $y = \dfrac{x-3}{2-x}$.

      The vertical asymptote is where the denominator is zero: $2-x=0$, so $x=2$.

      For the horizontal asymptote, divide numerator and denominator by $x$:
      $$y = \dfrac{1-\frac{3}{x}}{\frac{2}{x}-1}$$
      As $x \to \infty$,
      $$y \to \dfrac{1}{-1} = -1$$
      So the asymptotes are $x = 2$ and $y = -1$, option C.
