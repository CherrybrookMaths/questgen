      To translate left by $a$ units, replace $x$ by $x+a$. So $x$ becomes $x+\pi$.

      Hence $y=\ln x$ becomes
      $$y=\ln\left(\dfrac{x}{3}+\pi\right)$$

      The area of a sector is $A=\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta$, and $A=49$ cm$^2$.

      $$\begin{aligned} 49&=\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta \\ 98&=r^2\theta \\ \theta&=\dfrac{98}{r^2} \end{aligned}$$

      The arc length is

      $$l=r\theta=r\times\dfrac{98}{r^2}=\dfrac{98}{r}$$

      The perimeter is two radii plus the arc:

      $$P=2r+\dfrac{98}{r}=2r+98r^{-1}$$

      Differentiating:

      $$\dfrac{dP}{dr}=2-\dfrac{98}{r^2},\qquad \dfrac{d^2P}{dr^2}=\dfrac{196}{r^3}$$

      For a stationary point, $\dfrac{dP}{dr}=0$:

      $$\begin{aligned} 2-\dfrac{98}{r^2}&=0 \\ r^2&=49 \\ r&=7 \quad (r>0) \end{aligned}$$

      Nature: when $r=7$, $\dfrac{d^2P}{dr^2}=\dfrac{196}{7^3}>0$, so this is a minimum.

      The minimum perimeter occurs when $r=7$ cm, so the diameter is $14$ cm.

      Then $\theta=\dfrac{98}{7^2}=2$, so
