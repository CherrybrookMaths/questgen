      Differentiating,
      $$f'(x) = 2x + 12x^2$$

      $$\begin{aligned} 2x + 12x^2 &< 0 \\ 2x(1+6x) &< 0 \end{aligned}$$

      $$\therefore\ -\dfrac{1}{6} < x < 0$$
      This is option A.

        Using the product rule, $\dfrac{d}{dx}(uv)=u'v+uv'$, with
        $$\begin{aligned} u&=(x-2)^2 & v&=x+1\\ u'&=2(x-2) & v'&=1\end{aligned}$$
        $$\begin{aligned} f'(x) &= 2(x-2)(x+1) + (x-2)^2 \\ &= (x-2)(2x+2+x-2) \\ &= 3x(x-2) \end{aligned}$$
