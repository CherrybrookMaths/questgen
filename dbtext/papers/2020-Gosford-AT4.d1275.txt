        A stationary point occurs when $\dfrac{dV}{dr}=0$.
        $$300\pi r-\pi^2r^3=0$$
        $$\pi r\left(300-\pi r^2\right)=0$$

        $$r=\sqrt{\dfrac{300}{\pi}}=\dfrac{10\sqrt3}{\sqrt\pi}$$

        The gradient changes from positive to negative, so this is a maximum.
