      Let $x = BP$. Apply the sine rule.

      In the right-angled triangle with hypotenuse $BP$ and height $h$:
      $$\sin 10^\circ = \dfrac{h}{x}$$
      $$\begin{aligned} h &= x\sin 10^\circ \\ &= \dfrac{2\sin 4^\circ}{\sin 6^\circ} \times \sin 10^\circ \\ &\approx 0.2318 \text{ km} \end{aligned}$$
      $$\therefore\ h \approx 232 \text{ m}$$
