# Tag spec (prompt_version = tag-v2.2), 12 Nov 2026

One spec (TAG_SPEC v1 and v2 merged 12 Nov; v1 is in archive/specs/). The 2017 code is no longer asked for: old 2017 tags stay in the database as they are.
Tag NSW HSC Mathematics Advanced question PARTS. The "Owner rulings" at the end override anything above them.
Inputs: work/skills/dotpoints.json (359 dot points: id, focus_area, sub, text), work/skills/skills_spec.json (159 skills: skill, year, topic, counts_when, not_when, dot_points), work/skills/skill_prereqs.json (skill -> requires).

For each part give:
1. **dot_points**: every 2024 dot point the marks assess, each with role "primary" (exactly one: what most of the marks are for) or "also" (anything else a student needs to earn the marks). Test: "would a student lose marks without this?" Routine algebra and Year 11 basics a Year 12 student is assumed to have are not tagged on Year 12 parts.
   Primary rules: (a) marks decide; (b) a "hence" part belongs to what it asks for; (c) an application beats a technique (motion, area, optimisation, rates: the application dot point is primary, the calculus is "also"); (d) on a tie, the final answer decides.
   If the content has no 2024 dot point (removed: regression, Pareto, 3D trig, interest-factor tables...), dot_points = [] and give removed_reason and topic_2017 (the 2017 topic it belongs to).
2. **required_skills**: every skill (exact names from skills_spec.json) whose counts_when is met. Follow not_when. Don't add a skill only because it is a prerequisite of another tagged skill (prerequisites are applied automatically).
3. **can_use_skills**: other skills that are a valid alternative method for the same marks (only when the question leaves the method free). Usually empty.
4. **confidence** 0-1 for the part overall, and **note** for anything you were unsure about.
Output per part: {"part_id", "dot_points":[{"id","role"}], "required_skills":[...], "can_use_skills":[...], "removed_reason":"", "topic_2017":"", "confidence", "note"}.

## Clarifications (v2.1, from the 12 Nov pilot)
- **Lead-in parts** (a part with no marks whose sub-parts carry the marks, e.g. "Q14(c)" with (i), (ii)): tag nothing on the lead-in (dot_points [], skills []); its sub-parts carry the tags.
- **Assumed algebra (owner 12 Nov: "Yep that is fine")**: exactly these 12 skills are tagged ONLY when the part is about them (the marks are for that skill itself), never as a side step: Expanding and factorising, Algebraic fractions, Index laws, Surds and rationalising denominators, Linear equations and inequalities, Quadratic equations, Simultaneous equations, Function notation, Interval notation, Exact trigonometric ratios, Differentiation of powers of x, Logarithm laws. EVERY other skill is tagged whenever its counts_when is met, on any part, Year 11 or Year 12 — ignore any older wording in a skill's rule that says "never tagged on a part needing a Year 12 skill". Dot points follow the same rule: the matching Year 11 dot point of an assumed-algebra skill is tagged only when that skill is.
- **Prerequisites vs rules:** tag a skill whenever its own counts_when is met, even if it is also a prerequisite of another tagged skill, UNLESS a tagged skill's not_when says "do not also tag" it.
- **Primary tie-break:** "an application beats a technique" wins over "the final answer decides" (finding k inside a model: the model's dot point is primary).
- **Year of a part:** a part's year is the HIGHEST year among its dot points and skills (owner 12 Nov: "Y11 Qs can never be tagged with Y12 tags"). Tag what the marks need; if anything is Year 12, the part counts as a Year 12 question and never appears in Year 11-only filters.
- **Removed content format:** removed_reason = one line; topic_2017 = the 2017 code, e.g. "MA-S2.2" (no name).
- **Standard content**: add "standard_topic" (e.g. "MST-12-S2-03") for content that is in the Standard course (annuity tables), else "".
- **MCQ with picture options**: if the options are images or missing, use the stem, the solution and the correct answer to decide the method.
- **y = ka^t**: continuous-time models -> Exponential and logarithmic models; a quantity changing by a fixed factor each period, written as a sequence -> Geometric growth and decay.

### Syllabus scope, skills and gating (12 Nov)
- **Rational functions (owner 12 Nov 2026: "Yep thats fine" to the proposal below).** General rational functions (y = p(x)/q(x): asymptotes, sketching, inequalities from the graph) ARE Advanced: tag Y12-296 (its text includes "any vertical asymptotes or other discontinuities ... behaviour as x→±∞"), plus the calculus dot points used; hyperbolas y = a/(x−h)+k → Y11-038 / Y11-167. ONLY "sketch y = 1/f(x) from the graph of y = f(x)" for a general f is removed (2024 reciprocal limited to k/x). Never mark a rational-function part "removed" for being rational.
- **Absolute value of general functions (owner 12 Nov 2026: "rule the same as rational").** Questions on |f(x)| for non-linear f (e.g. |x ln x²| = k, |sin 2x| period) ARE Advanced: tag the dot points the question actually uses (graphical solution Y11-034, trig graph transformations Y12-203, etc.), never "removed" for being |f(x)|.
- **Year 11 content is examinable (NESA: "The Mathematics Advanced Year 11 course will be assumed knowledge for this examination and may be examined").** Coverage "moved_to_year_11" is displayed as "Year 11 (assumed knowledge, may be examined)" and is never filtered out of exam building.
- **Courses (owner 12 Nov 2026: "We will grow this bank into standard and extension later").** Keep Standard papers; questions carry their course. Advanced-overlap questions in Standard papers are tagged to Advanced dot points; Standard-only content keeps its Standard topic.
- **Dot points are shown to teachers by subheading and a short name, never the internal id (owner 12 Nov: "Yes").** Short names are written during the re-tag.
- **Method and context both (owner 12 Nov 2026: "do both for this").** Annuity / loan "show that" parts carry the context skill (Annuities, Reducing balance loans) AND the method skill (Sum of a GP, Recurrence relations for balances).
- **Gating by what has been learnt (owner 12 Nov 2026: "skills need to be gated like that"; e.g. "if theyve done diff they can't do a diff question which contains int until they do int").** A part is shown only when every one of its SKILLS has been learnt (owner 12 Nov: "the requirement for questions should not be full topics, but specific skills"); "learnt" is a list of skills (ticking a topic ticks its skills). Dot points do not gate. So also-assessed dot points and skills must be COMPLETE: tag every skill the marks need (no 1–4 cap), except Year 11 basics a Year 12 student is assumed to have. Who sets "learnt" (student or teacher) is undecided.
- **Skill list settled (owner 12 Nov 2026: "1. drop, 2. that's fine 3. fine as well").** 160 skills, formal textbook-heading names, no leading "The", casual/student wording kept as aliases. Final list: work/skills/skills_final.json. Names change only if the pilot shows a problem (they will anchor "learnt" lists).
- **Skill prerequisites (owner 12 Nov 2026: "do it").** Each skill lists the skills it always builds on (e.g. Solving optimisation problems → Stationary points and their nature). Gating checks a part's skills PLUS their prerequisites, so tags stay lean ("not when" rules may say "don't also tag") without showing questions too early. File: work/skills/skill_prereqs.json.
- **Annuity tables moved to Standard (owner 12 Nov: "move it to std").** 159 Advanced skills; work/skills/standard_skills.json starts the Standard list.
- **"Can use" skills (owner 12 Nov 2026: "yeah I guess we can try that").** Skills come in two kinds: REQUIRED (the marks can't be earned without it; gates the question) and CAN USE (a valid alternative method the student may choose, e.g. Second derivative test when any method is allowed; shows the question in that skill's practice/search, never gates).
- **Year of a part (owner 12 Nov 2026: "Y11 Qs can never be tagged with Y12 tags").** A part's year is the highest year among its tags. A part needing any Year 12 dot point or skill counts as Year 12 and never appears in Year 11-only selections (old-syllabus papers sometimes set now-Year-12 content, e.g. derivatives of eˣ, in Year 11).

- **Free keywords dropped.** v1 `free_tags` are replaced by skills (required / can use) and the skills' aliases for search.
- **"Also" dot points kept (owner 12 Nov: "Yep but confirm we are keeping it").** Taggers keep giving every "also" dot point. They do not gate or file questions (skills gate, the primary dot point files), but they are kept for exact syllabus-coverage reports, stability when the skill list changes, and an automatic cross-check (an also dot point with no matching skill, or a skill with no matching dot point, flags a likely tagging error). The owner does not review them: the Tags step shows them collapsed.
- **Owner picks topics, not gates (owner 12 Nov: "I don't feel like I can accurately choose the gates" -> "yeah lets do that").** Where tagger and checker disagree, the owner chooses only the main dot point (or removed). Gates are settled automatically: skills both named stay; each skill only one side named is kept only if a solution step needs it (quoted evidence, work/gates/out_*.json), else dropped or moved to can-use. Missing gates are the worse error (a student sees a question they can't do yet), so when unsure, keep. The owner spot-checks about 20 gates with their evidence when the student side is built.
- **One topic home per question (owner 12 Nov: "a questions parts shouldn't be split if they're related" -> "Yeah lets do that").** A question is one unit and is filed under ONE topic: its home = the dot-point subheading carrying the most marks across its parts' main dot points (computed; on a tie in marks the LATER part's topic wins, owner 12 Nov on QID 1221: "this should've been in integration"; the owner overrides in Review > Tags, stored as reviews/q<id>.home = 'A' | 'B' | {id} | {cov}). Other parts' topics become gates only, never extra filings. Taggers still tag part by part (it keeps gates complete); future imports also state the home topic and a mismatch with the computed home is flagged. Unrelated old-format parts are split into separate questions at import, so the rule holds for them too.
- **"Hence integrate" questions go to integration (owner 12 Nov: "yeah do that", after reviewing 18 "hence" questions).** If the last part integrates using a derivative found in an earlier part ("show d/dx(...) = ..., hence find ∫..."), the home is the integration topic, whatever the marks. A general "final hence part decides" rule was rejected: in most other "hence" questions (hence state the maximum, hence solve the inequality) the earlier part is the real topic.

### Rule fixes, 12 Nov evening (owner: "go ahead with the rule fix as well")
- **Product, quotient and chain rule dot points.** Any exponential, logarithm or trig function involved -> Y12-240. Otherwise, two or more rules combined, or the student must choose the rule -> Y11-134. Otherwise the single rule -> Y11-131 (product), Y11-132 (quotient), Y11-133 (chain). Examples: x²(3x − 1)⁴ -> Y11-134; x²eˣ -> Y12-240; (2x + 1)⁵ -> Y11-133. The skills (Product rule, Quotient rule, Chain rule) are tagged as before.
- **Graphs of sec, cosec and cot (owner: "with a note").** No 2024 dot point names them. Untransformed: main dot point Y11-088 with note "reciprocal trig graph: no 2024 dot point"; transformed: Y12-203 with the same note. Skill: Graphs of trigonometric functions (widened to include them).
- **3D questions are not Advanced at all (owner 12 Nov: "no 3D questions in course for Adv, all in extension 1").** A question set in three dimensions (towers seen from two points, pyramids, prisms, points above level ground) is removed as a whole, including any part that is plain 2D trigonometry or calculus. This replaces the earlier part-by-part rule. A question that was only flagged 3D by mistake (e.g. a split Q14 part on another topic) is tagged normally.
- **Recurrence relations (owner: "included").** The skill "Recurrence relations for loans and annuities" is renamed "Recurrence relations" and covers any recurrence (populations, doses, repeated percentage change), not only finance.
- **New skill: Integral as a function of its upper limit (Y12-257) (owner: "sure").** For d/dx ∫ₐˣ f(t)dt, A(x) as a primitive, or features of A(x) read from the graph of f. Requires Definite integrals.
- **Every paper in the bank is old-syllabus (owner 12 Nov: "2027 hsc will be the first new hsc year").** HSC papers and school tasks up to and including 2026 were set under the 2017 syllabus. Tagging places each question in the 2024 course; that a school or NESA set a topic before 2027 is never evidence that it is in the 2024 course.
- **Standalone limits are kept (owner 12 Nov: "limits keep").** A question that only evaluates a limit (e.g. by factorising) is in the course, filed under Y11-122 (derivative from first principles), with its algebra skill as the gate.
